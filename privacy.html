Privacy Policy

Time Imposter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Player names and game settings are stored only on your own device and are never sent anywhere. The app makes no network requests except to Apple's App Store, which is required to process the in-app purchase.

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
